# Critique c1

Reviewer stance: learning scientist + Cambridge International Principal Examiner (9609 P4 / 9709). Focus: §04, §05, §07, §13, and the pedagogy-bearing parts of §02, §10, §15.A. Verified against `designs/SYNTHESIS.md` and `research/{exam-systems,gap-marking-and-cambridge,learning-science}.md`.

## Verdict

I would not sign this off as-is, though the architecture is right and most of it is better than anything shipping today. Three things must change before an agent starts building. First, the product's central behavioural promise contradicts itself: §04 declares "never a refusal in prose" and §13 gates refusal-in-prose at zero, yet the document's own flagship dialogue D3 refuses a 20-mark model answer in prose — that is the single most-requested action in the product and it is currently a spec that fails its own eval (F01). Second, the Mark-Yield formula cancels the learner's own gain headroom (m_s is proportional to G_s), so the planner will systematically top up nearly-mastered topics and starve weak and unseen ones — the opposite of exam coaching, and the worked example celebrates the bug as a feature (F02). Third, the marking-calibration story is not statistically sound: temperature 0 with a pinned seed makes n=3 self-consistency vacuous while the worked example prints three different totals (F03); QWK gates are computed on ~20 scripts per question family, where the 95% CI is roughly ±0.25 (F08); and the fixed-window seed alarm will false-pause constantly (F30). Beyond those, several marking rules would not survive a standardisation meeting: AO4 capped to Level 1 by a pre-check rather than levelled from its own grid (F04), no within-band placement rule for a five-mark-wide AO3 band (F15), and two worked-example fixtures that violate the contracts they are supposed to fix in CI (F18–F22). §15.B.1 — the rubric template the renderer snapshot test depends on — is an empty code fence (F05). Fix F01–F09 and the eleven contradictions, and this becomes signable.

## Top 10 (ranked)

1. **F01** "Just the answer" on a levels Item is a prose refusal — spec fails 04-CORE-004 and 13-PED-012 in its own fixture D3.
2. **F02** Mark-Yield: `m_s ∝ G_s` cancels gain headroom; the planner never invests in weak or unseen topics.
3. **F03** Temperature 0 + pinned seed vs n=3 self-consistency: mutually exclusive; the 05.12 fixture prints `[11, 11, 12]`.
4. **F08** Gold-set arithmetic is infeasible; QWK gated on ~20 scripts per family is noise, not a gate.
5. **F04** AO4 capped at Level 1 by `command_word_check` instead of levelled from the AO4 grid — systematic under-marking; §15.A.3 says Level 2 for the same case.
6. **F05** §15.B.1 is an empty ```yaml fence; 15-RUBR-003 cites a §15.B.5 that does not exist.
7. **F07** FSRS desired retention 0.95 is not achievable inside the session budget; backlog rolls over silently and no one is told.
8. **F06** The 90-second time-box opens rung 5 with no `seen_solution` tag and no retest — a designed label failure against 13-PED-011 = 0.
9. **F10 / F11** Calibration and FSRS acceptance metrics are backwards: Brier ≤0.15 punishes underconfidence; log loss ≤0.35 is worse than the base rate.
10. **F17 / F16** "Upheld" means opposite things in §05 and §13; item tolerances disagree between 05-MARK-009 and 13-MARK-013.
