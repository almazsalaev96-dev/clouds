# Critique c2

*Lens: principal product designer (frontier chat craft) + a 17-year-old A-level student who currently uses ChatGPT, SME and PMT. Focus: §03 parity, §09 design system, §10 screens, §02 personas/scenarios, §15.E microcopy, UI-facing rules elsewhere.*

## Verdict

No, I would not sign off yet — not because the design is wrong but because the design system contradicts its own CI gates in four places where the gates are declared unwaivable, and because one safety surface is literally missing from the file. Three things must change before an agent starts building: (1) the crisis-resources config in §10.24 is an empty, unterminated ```yaml fence — the helpline table the distress flow depends on does not exist, and the unclosed fence corrupts markdown parsing for the remaining 2,800 lines; (2) the colour tokens `--border`, `--border-strong` and `--warning` cannot pass 09-A11Y-005/09-QA-016 at the luminances given, and the citation chip's 11 px numeral cannot pass 09-QA-003, so `pixel-gate` can never go green as written; (3) §03 and §09 publish two incompatible keyboard maps, and the digit keys 1/2/3 are bound to three different things while 10-SESN-001 guarantees they can never fire for the intent row. Beyond the mechanics, the product has one structural hole and one tonal one. The hole: there is no **Ask** screen — no route, no palette verb, no entry point, no empty state — even though §00, §03 and §10.3 all reference "Ask" as the answer to "a conversation without a Course". That is the "I just want help now" moment, and it is the single most common thing a student does; today the fastest path to it in Margin is undefined. The tonal one: the product forces three compulsory metacognitive gates — mandatory confidence before Submit, mandatory predict before a paper, mandatory postdict before *any* mark is visible — which is the one place this stops feeling like a tutor and starts feeling like a worksheet, and it contradicts 04-CORE-004's own promise that nothing is ever withheld behind a demand. Everything else on this list is fixable in a day of editing; those five are not optional.

## Top 10 (ranked)

1. **C2-01** — §10.24's crisis-resource YAML is empty and its fence unterminated: the safety surface has no data and the file's markdown breaks from line 5338 on.
2. **C2-03 / C2-04 / C2-05** — `--border` (1.5:1), `--border-strong` (1.9:1) and `--warning` (≈1.9:1) fail the 3:1 gate they are used for; the 11 px citation numeral fails the 12 px floor. `pixel-gate` is unwaivable for these rows, so nothing ships.
3. **C2-11** — There is no **Ask** screen: no §10 subsection, no route in 10-CONV-003, no palette verb, no Home entry. The core chat loop's default action is unspecified.
4. **C2-06 / C2-08** — Two contradictory keyboard maps (§03 vs §09) and 1/2/3 triple-bound; the intent chips' shortcut can never fire given 10-SESN-001.
5. **C2-02** — Break-reminder threshold is 45 / 50 / 40 / "every 25" across four sections. Teen-safety parity is a non-negotiable with a CI fixture asserting 45:00.
6. **C2-09 / C2-10** — Two student-facing strings are factually false: "nothing from this session is saved" (30-day retention exists) and "marking limits are off" (Free is 6/day). Both violate 00-NON-017 / 01-PRIN-002.
7. **C2-13** — Compulsory predict, confidence and postdict. Postdict blocks *all* marks after a 90-minute mock. This is the flow a student rage-quits.
8. **C2-07** — Four different LCP budgets (1.5 / 1.8 / 2.0 / 2.5 s) and three CLS budgets for the same routes; the release gate is undecidable.
9. **C2-12 / C2-30 / C2-31** — Sign out, resume-after-Stop and any memory-written affordance are absent from §10 entirely.
10. **C2-16 / C2-14** — The dyslexia preset has two different definitions with different numbers, and one is asserted by a CI fixture; points papers must and must not carry a band.
