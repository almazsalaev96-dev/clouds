# §04 The learning core

## 04.1 Scope and the Attempt as the unit of learning

The learning core is the set of rules that turn a frontier model into a tutor that produces exam gains rather than practice inflation. It governs every Learn-mode and Practise-mode interaction inside a Course; Mark mode is specified in §05 and reuses only the feedback template (04.8) and the lint (04.8, 04.10) from here. Every rule in this section is enforced in code or in CI, never only in a prompt.

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-CORE-001 | The Attempt is the atomic record. Every tutoring interaction on a gradable Item creates or updates exactly one Attempt (schema in SYNTHESIS §3.1 and §07.1). A feature that does not create or improve Attempts is out of MVP scope. | Trace any MVP feature to the Attempt field it writes; reviewer rejects features with none. |
| 04-CORE-002 | Pedagogy lives in three enforced layers: (a) cached system instructions per mode (§15.A), (b) the Orchestrator that vets every draft Turn (04.10), (c) CI evals that gate every prompt, model or policy change (04.10, §13). A rule that exists only in layer (a) is a bug. | Each rule in this section maps to a check in (b) or (c). |
| 04-CORE-003 | The tutor never grades or guides on an Item without the key: every Item is pre-solved by a strong model (STEM: plus SymPy/sandbox verification; levels: plus the structured scheme and indicative content) before the first Turn, and the key is held in the Orchestrator context, never shown unless a rung-5 event fires. | Golden conversation: Item loaded → `presolve_status = verified` before TTFT; Items with `presolve_status ≠ verified` route to feedback-only mode (no marks, no confirmation of correctness). |
| 04-CORE-004 | Nothing in this section is ever a refusal in prose. Every withheld thing is replaced by a visible state (chip, tooltip, label) and an allowed alternative. | Adversarial suite: 0 Turns containing "I can't help with that" or equivalents; every gate state has a UI hook in §10. |

Why: Bastani et al. 2025 — unguarded GPT raised practice scores +48% and lowered exam scores; the guarded tutor raised practice +127% with no exam harm. Kestin 2025 and Tutor CoPilot both gave the tutor a verified key. LearnLM's developers' top request was instruction-following that survives student pressure — which prompts alone do not deliver.

## 04.2 Anatomy of a Turn

A Turn is one Learn-mode assistant message. It is short, does one thing, and ends by handing the work back.

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-TURN-001 | Length ≤120 words (whitespace tokens, excluding math/code blocks and citation chips) unless the student explicitly asked for depth in the current message or set the effort dial to Deep; hard ceiling 150 words even then. Marking cards (§05) are exempt. | Telemetry: <2% of Learn Turns >150 words; p50 ≤80 words. |
| 04-TURN-002 | Fixed internal order: (1) task-level acknowledgement of what was right, specific and unadorned, omitted when nothing was right; (2) one diagnosis, named with the misconception label from the Pack when one applies; (3) exactly one move — a question *or* a hint *or* a micro-example; (4) the hand-back. | Orchestrator structural parse tags each sentence; Turns with two moves or two questions regenerate (04.10). |
| 04-TURN-003 | The hand-back is behaviour, not text: after the Turn paints, the composer takes focus and its placeholder names the action ("Try the next line", "Write the counter-argument sentence", "Give the unit"). The Turn may end with the action as a plain imperative; it never ends with "Does that make sense?" or "Let me know if…". | Placeholder string is populated from `turn.handback_action` on 100% of Learn Turns; banned closers appear in 0% (lint). |
| 04-TURN-004 | Exactly one question mark addressed to the student per Turn. Rhetorical questions count. | Lint regex + LLM check; violations regenerate. |
| 04-TURN-005 | No person-praise, exclamation marks, emoji, "Great question", "Good job", "You've got this", or therapy-speak ("let's take a breath"). Task-specific acknowledgement is allowed: "Contribution per unit is right: $12." | Praise audit <5% (04.8); regex bans at 0%. |
| 04-TURN-006 | Never restate the whole problem; quote at most the clause under discussion. Never solve beyond the current step (leak, 04.10). | Leak rate <5% on the 30-turn adversarial suite. |
| 04-TURN-007 | Every Turn carries metadata: `{rung, intent, move_type ∈ {question, hint, micro_example, deliver, redirect}, misconception_id?, handback_action, lint_result, model, cache_hit, latency_ms, policy_version}`. | Langfuse trace shows all fields on 100% of Turns. |
| 04-TURN-008 | Register: "an examiner who is on your side" — declarative, concrete, second person, exam vocabulary (AO, command word, tariff, level) used without apology. British spelling for Cambridge Courses; the student's L1 may be used for explanation when the Course sets `explain_language`, but exam vocabulary and any answer text stay in the exam language. | Voice preset Examiner/Coach/Plain (§03) all pass the same lint. |

Why: students' second-most-hated trait is sycophancy and the top formatting complaint is "walls of text"; feedback works through information content (Wisniewski 2020, d=0.48), and person-praise is among the weakest forms.

## 04.3 The Effort Gate

The Effort Gate withholds solution content on a gradable Item until the student has produced bounded effort. It is a state on the Attempt, rendered as UI (§10), never argued in prose.

**What counts as an attempt (any one satisfies the gate):**

| Item type | Satisfies the gate |
|---|---|
| Numeric / STEM | A final value (with or without units), or a first line of working, or a named method ("I'd use contribution per unit") |
| Short-answer / definition | A definition or a one-sentence answer, even wrong |
| Levels-based essay (8–20 marks) | A plan of ≥2 bullet points, or a first paragraph, or a stated judgement |
| Multiple choice (MCQ) | A chosen option **plus one sentence naming why one other option is wrong** — the elimination sentence is the attempt, and it is the highest-value MCQ study behaviour (04-MCQ-001) |
| Mechanism / diagram | A photographed or drawn sketch, or a first step in words ("nucleophile attacks the carbonyl carbon") |
| Practical / planning | An independent variable and one controlled variable, or a first step of the method |
| Any | "I don't know where to start" **plus one sentence of what they do know** about the topic. A bare "idk" is not a refusal point: it receives **one** rung-1 question first ("One sentence on what you do know is enough — what is the question asking for?"); a second bare "idk", or 20 s of no input after the first, opens the time-box automatically with the chip visible (04-GATE-002). |

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-GATE-001 | `attempt.gate_state ∈ {open, satisfied, timeboxed, bypassed_answer}`. Solution content (rungs 4–5, correctness confirmation, "Just the answer") is only emitted when state ≠ `open`. | Orchestrator blocks rung ≥4 and any `confirm` move while `open`; unit tests per Item type. |
| 04-GATE-002 | UI hook: a "Your go first" chip beneath the Item (§10.5 Session, §10.6 Practise runner) with a secondary "Take 90 seconds with help" option. The time-box is **graded help, not dead waiting**: rung-2 hint → 45 s → rung-3 hint → 45 s → the composer asks for one line, "what you tried, or what is blocking you". That line satisfies the gate (`satisfied`, `gate_reason = timebox_line`) and the ladder continues at rung 4. Pressing through without the line sets `timeboxed` and runs the **whole** 04-INTN-002 script — rung 5, `seen_solution = true`, the "Seen solution · retest {weekday}" chip, a forced retest at 24–72 h and the near-transfer Item — so the time-box is never the cheap path: it costs 90 s and carries the same label and retest load as "Just the answer". | Chip present on 100% of gradable Items with `gate_state = open`; 100% of `timeboxed` Attempts carry `seen_solution = true` and a scheduled retest; suite case `timebox_farm` (10 consecutive time-box exits) produces the same labelling and retest load as 10 "Just the answer" presses; suite case `timebox_line` ends at rung 4 with no seen-solution label. |
| 04-GATE-003 | The gate never blocks rungs 1–3. A student who has not attempted can always receive a metacognitive prompt, a conceptual pointer or the next step. | Adversarial suite: rungs 1–3 available at `open`. |
| 04-GATE-004 | Exceptions where the gate is pre-satisfied: (a) Item is a non-gradable Ask (no Course) — no gate; (b) the student is in Mark mode with their own answer pasted — the answer is the attempt; (c) a worked example shown deliberately at fading stage 1 (04.7) — the self-explanation checkpoint replaces the gate; (d) intent = Check my steps with steps supplied. | Each exception sets `gate_state = satisfied` with `gate_reason`. |
| 04-GATE-005 | Never a refusal sentence, never a lecture about learning, never rate-limited, never harder to pass over time. If asked "are you withholding the answer?", answer yes in one sentence with the deal ("Yes — one attempt or a plan first, then it's yours, and one like it after."). | Lint bans "I can't give you the answer"; honesty test in the suite. |
| 04-GATE-006 | Rule A5 — never confirm a bare guess. "Is it B?" receives one question for the reason; a stated reason (right or wrong) satisfies the gate for that Item. | Suite case `bare_guess` passes 100%. |
| 04-GATE-007 | Log `gate_latency_ms` (Item shown → first student token) and `gate_path ∈ {attempt, plan, dont_know_plus_sentence, timebox_line, timebox_through, pre_satisfied}` on the Attempt; these feed the debt guard (04.11). | Fields non-null on all gated Attempts. |
| 04-GATE-008 | **Gate quality.** The gate has a quality test, not only a presence test. The same Haiku-class classifier that produces `unmarkable` in Practise tags every gate-satisfying input `gate_quality ∈ {substantive, token}`. `token` — lorem, "idk idk", the question text pasted back, two empty bullets, a single character, a copy of the tutor's own last Turn — opens rungs 1–4 but never rung 5 and never "Just the answer"; the student is shown the time-box chip instead, never a refusal sentence. `substantive` opens everything. The classification is stored on the Attempt and is a debt-guard signal (04.11), not a punishment. | Fixture set of 40 token attempts → 0 rung-5 deliveries and 40 time-box offers; 40 genuine but wrong attempts → 40 gate opens; classifier precision ≥0.9 and recall ≥0.85 on the labelled set; 0 Turns containing a refusal string. |
| 04-GATE-009 | The time-box is the **fourth** named path past the gate and is registered as one rather than hidden inside a UI affordance: §00.2's non-negotiable reads "an Attempt, a plan, 'don't know where to start' plus one sentence, **or a completed 90-second time-box**", and this row and that wording change in the same PR. It is an untested deviation from the guarded-tutor design the gate rests on (04.1), so it ships behind an experiment arm: 13-EFF-003 gains a `time_box on/off` arm, primary outcome 28-day unaided retention, guard-rail 30-day product retention. | The arm exists in the §13 experiment registry before launch; §00.2 and 04-GATE-002 name the same four paths; a CI check asserts the two strings match. |

Why: "bounded productive effort before strong assistance" (Brcic & Frljic 2026) is the compromise that keeps the Bastani result without the churn of a refusing tutor ("annoys you into learning"). A gate that tests only whether *something* was typed is not that compromise, which is why 04-GATE-008 exists.

## 04.4 The Help Ladder and the entry rung

Five rungs, one rung per Turn, the ladder resets per Item, and the rung reached is stored on the Attempt. Rung definitions differ by Item type; the numbers do not.

| Rung | Numeric / STEM | Short-answer / definition | Levels-based essay | Multiple choice (MCQ) | Mechanism / diagram | Practical / planning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Metacognitive | "Which concept family — costing, cash or profit?" | "What is the command word asking for?" | "Which AO does *Evaluate* reach that *Analyse* does not?" | "Which concept is this question testing?" | "What is the nucleophile here, and what is it attacking?" | "What is the one variable you change?" |
| 2 Conceptual pointer (principle, no numbers) | "Break-even uses contribution per unit, not price." | "A definition needs the class and the distinguishing feature." | "Application means facts true of *this* business, not the sector." | "One of these options is the classic reversal of the rule. Which family is it in?" | "Arrows start at electron pairs, never at atoms." | "Control what would otherwise change the dependent variable." |
| 3 Procedural (next step only) | "Subtract variable cost per unit from price." | "Start with 'the total value of…'." | "Your first chain: name the cause, then the mechanism, then the effect on *this* firm." | "Eliminate the two that contradict the stem." | "Draw the first curly arrow from the lone pair." | "Write the method step that fixes temperature." |
| 4 Partial worked step with a blank | "Contribution = 20 − 8 = 12; break-even = 36,000 ÷ ___" | Half the definition given, the distinguishing feature blank | One model **paragraph** one level above the student's current AO level, with the evaluative or linking sentence blanked; never a model essay | Two options remain; one discriminating fact is given ("stock is a flow only if it is measured over a period") and the student picks | The intermediate with one arrow missing, drawn by the deterministic renderer | The method with one control step blank |
| 5 Full solution + near-transfer | Verified full working, then a numbers-changed item done unaided | Full definition, then a sibling term to define unaided | 8–12 marks: full paragraph set for one AO chain; 20 marks: a **plan** (define → two context-linked chains → counter → weighted judgement) plus one exemplar paragraph, then a new context to plan unaided | The key, plus why each distractor is wrong named to its misconception, then a sibling item unaided | Full mechanism from the renderer, then a homologous mechanism unaided | Full plan, then a variant investigation unaided |

**Rung 4a — micro-worked chain (levels Items only).** Three labelled sentences — cause → mechanism → effect on this firm — ≤45 words in total, with the third sentence blanked, followed by one self-explanation prompt ("Why does the third sentence have to name the firm?"). It is the entry rung for a novice on a levels Item. The novice's problem with rung 4 is the *size* of a model paragraph, not the *level* of assistance, so 4a keeps the assistance high and shrinks the artefact; dropping the novice to rung 3 would run expertise reversal backwards.

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-LADR-001 | Entry rung = f(**unaided** mastery `M_unaided` of the Item's primary syllabus point, Item type). `M_unaided` (07-MAST-001b) is computed over Attempts with `rung_reached ≤ 1`, `seen_solution = false` and `intent ≠ just_answer` — never over the hint-inflated `M_s`, so a student whose competence is scaffold-dependent keeps the scaffold. Bands: `M_unaided` <0.4 → rung 4, and **rung 4a** (the micro-worked chain above) on levels Items; 0.4–0.7 → rung 2; >0.7 → rung 1, with rung 4 refused until two failed attempts on this Item ("Two tries first — you are close on this one."). Exam week (T−7 per component): Secure+ points never receive rung 4. | Unit tests over the (`M_unaided`, type) grid; `attempt.entry_rung` logged; a student with 20 rung-4 correct and 0 unaided correct still enters at rung 4 (4a on levels). |
| 04-LADR-002 | Climb one rung per Turn, only on a failed or absent attempt after the previous rung; never skip; never descend within an Item. "Teach me" pressed again = one rung up. | Suite: rung sequence strictly increasing by 1 per Item. |
| 04-LADR-003 | Rung 5 always includes the near-transfer Item immediately after (same skill, changed numbers/context, generated per 05-GEN-007 in §05.10), and the Attempt on the original is tagged `seen_solution = true`. A rung-5 correct contributes 0 to mastery. | 100% of rung-5 events create a follow-up Item; `seen_solution` set. |
| 04-LADR-004 | Levels Items never receive a model essay at any rung **before** the student's own Marked Attempt on that Item. After it, the model answer is available under 04-INTN-007 and is delivered through Papers (§05.9) with the seen-solution script. The limit is always a chip state (04.5), never a sentence of policy. | Lint: no Turn on a levels Item with no Marked Attempt exceeds 220 words of continuous answer prose; suite case `give_me_the_essay` passes; suite case `demand_essay_after_mark` delivers the model answer. |
| 04-LADR-006 | A **plan** is a first-class attempt artefact, not only a rung-5 output. The composer accepts a submitted plan on any levels Item; it satisfies the gate (`gate_reason = plan`) and routes to the plan Mark form (05-GEN-011, `item_form: "plan"`, scored against the AO3 and AO4 evidence rules only, no AO1/AO2 credit). Rung 5's plan and the student's own plan use the same shape, so the difference the student sees is structural, not stylistic. Where a Pack's paper has no plan form yet, a submitted plan receives ladder feedback and no number — never a silent refusal to accept it. | A submitted plan sets `gate_state = satisfied`, `gate_reason = plan`; where the plan form exists the plan is Marked against AO3/AO4 only; where it does not, no band and no mark render. |
| 04-LADR-005 | Rung reached is visible to the student in the message margin (§10.5) and to teachers only as class aggregates (§07.9). | UI check; teacher API returns no per-Turn rung. |

Why: expertise reversal — low prior knowledge gains from high assistance (d=+0.505), high prior knowledge is harmed by it (d=−0.428). The novice therefore gets the *most* worked support, sized to what they can parse (rung 4a), not the least. Paragraph-not-essay keeps AO4 judgement the student's own work, and driving the entry rung from unaided mastery stops hinted success from removing the hints that produced it.

## 04.5 Intent chips and "Just the answer"

Three chips render as a reply row under the tutor's Turn while a gradable Item is open — never in the composer, which carries only the mode chip (§09).

| Chip | Available | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Teach me** | Always | Climb the ladder from the entry rung (04.4). |
| **Check my steps** | Always | The tutor asks for the student's steps or answer first if absent; then confirms or corrects with reasoning, quoting the first wrong step; no unsolicited continuation. Default intent in exam week. |
| **Just the answer** | After one attempt, a plan, or the "don't know + one sentence" path; before that, present but disabled with tooltip "After one attempt." `gate_quality = token` (04-GATE-008) keeps it disabled with the tooltip "One real line first." | Fixed script below (04-INTN-002). |
| **Full plan + one paragraph** | The same chip, relabelled, on a levels Item of ≥8 marks with no Marked Attempt yet. Tooltip: "Model essays unlock after your own marked attempt." | Delivers rung 5 for the tariff (plan plus one exemplar paragraph) under the 04-INTN-002 script and **no scope sentence** — the label is the whole explanation. |
| **Model answer** | The same chip again, once the student's own Attempt on that past-paper Item has been Marked (04-INTN-007). | Delivers the full model answer under the 04-INTN-002 script. |

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-INTN-001 | Chips are remembered per Course and per Item type ("Check my steps" for 0580 numeric, "Teach me" for 9609 essays) and pre-selected next time; one tap changes it. | Preference persisted in Learner Graph `prefs.intent_by_item_type`. |
| 04-INTN-002 | **"Just the answer" script, fixed:** (1) one sentence naming the deal, only the first time in a Session: "Here it is — and one like it right after so it sticks."; (2) deliver rung 5 for the Item type; (3) tag the Attempt `seen_solution = true`, render the label chip from §15.E `retest.scheduled` ("Seen solution · retest {weekday}") with the actual weekday; (4) schedule an unaided retest at 24–72 h (FSRS `due` override, 04.12); (5) present the near-transfer Item. Never refused, never rate-limited, never lectured, never a second sentence about learning. | Suite: 100% of eligible requests answered within one Turn; retest scheduled 100%; label rendered; words before the answer ≤20. |
| 04-INTN-003 | If asked whether the answer was withheld or why the label exists, answer honestly in one sentence. Never deny the gate. | Suite honesty case. |
| 04-INTN-004 | Homework-due-in-10-minutes is a legitimate intent: deliver the answer under the same script; the retest is scheduled for tomorrow **instead of** the near-transfer Item now — a student ten minutes from a deadline is not served a second question — and the Session end card says so in one line: "One like this tomorrow, 07:30 — it takes two minutes." | Case `deadline_pressure`: no near-transfer Item is served in the Session, a retest Card exists with `retest_at` in 20–26 h, and 0 Turns contain a lecture. |
| 04-INTN-005 | A seen-solution Item never counts toward Secure; the retest, if correct unaided, counts as one of the two required. | Mastery unit test. |
| 04-INTN-006 | A student who presses "Just the answer" on ≥60% of gradable Items over a rolling 7 days is not blocked; the signal feeds the debt guard (04.11) and nothing else. | No code path reduces availability of the chip. |
| 04-INTN-007 | **Model answers after a Mark.** Once the student's own Attempt on a past-paper Item has been Marked (§05), a full model answer for that Item is available on that Item and is delivered under the 04-INTN-002 script: `seen_solution = true`, the label chip, a retest at 24–72 h, and a fresh context to plan unaided instead of a numbers-changed twin. Nothing about the limit is ever explained in prose: the chip's own label carries the state, and the only permitted sentence is the honest one-liner of 04-INTN-003 when the student asks directly. | Suite case `demand_essay_after_mark`: the model answer is delivered inside one Turn, `seen_solution` and the retest are set, and 0 Turns contain scope prose. Suite case `give_me_the_essay` (no Mark yet): 0 model-essay tokens and the chip renders "Full plan + one paragraph". |

**MCQ technique.** 9708 P1/P3 and the IGCSE MCQ components are gradable Items like any other; the gate row (04.3) and the ladder column (04.4) cover the interaction, and one rule covers the exam technique.

## 04.6 Misconception diagnosis

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-MISC-001 | Every MVP syllabus point ships with ≥3 misconceptions and ≥2 diagnostic Items whose distractors map one-to-one to those misconceptions. Schema: `misconception {id, syllabus_point_id, label (≤6 words), description, er_citations[{series, paraphrase}], contrast_pair_item_ids[], prerequisite_point_id?, prevalence_prior}`. | Pack gate `gold-seeded` fails below the counts (§06). |
| 04-MISC-002 | Mining pipeline (batch, §08): for each examiner report, extract sentences of the form "candidates (often/frequently/commonly) …", "a common error was …", "weaker responses …", "few candidates …"; cluster by syllabus point; a human reviewer names the label and writes a contrast pair; prevalence prior = share of series in which the pattern recurs. Store our paraphrase and the citation, never verbatim ER text beyond §06 extract limits. | Compiler review UI shows ER sentence → label → contrast pair; ≥80% first-pass acceptance by the reviewer. |
| 04-MISC-003 | Detection on a wrong or partial answer: (a) distractor match — the answer equals a mapped distractor; (b) pattern match — the presolve stores expected wrong values (inverted ratio, missing ×100, profit-for-cash) and the answer matches one; (c) language match for levels Items — the AO2 context detector or evaluation-in-Analyse detector (§05) fires. A match sets `attempt.misconception_candidate`. | Precision ≥0.8 on the tagged eval set per subject. |
| 04-MISC-004 | Confirmation before naming: the tutor asks for the reasoning trace once ("Talk me through how you got 42."); the trace is classified **slip / misconception / missing prerequisite** by a Haiku-class classifier with the candidate in context; confidence <0.6 → one more diagnostic Item, never a second question in the same Turn. | Classifier agreement with teacher labels ≥0.75 κ. |
| 04-MISC-005 | Routing: slip → quick fix and continue; misconception → contrast case (two Items differing in one feature: "Both firms sell 1,000 units; one has a loan repayment this month. Which has lower profit, which lower cash?") and the misconception is named to the student in its label; missing prerequisite → detour to the prerequisite node, one Item there, then return; the prerequisite is scheduled (04.12). | Suite cases per route; `attempt.error_class` set. |
| 04-MISC-006 | Confirmed misconceptions write to the Learner Graph (`misconceptions[]`, §07) with `status ∈ {suspected, confirmed, resolved}`; resolved requires two unaided correct on contrast Items ≥2 days apart; the tutor's context carries the student's open misconceptions so the same trap is targeted, not re-explained. | Graph write on confirm; read in context (§07.3). |
| 04-MISC-007 | 9609 seed list (minimum): revenue vs profit; cash flow vs profit; business name as "application"; evaluation inside Analyse Items; inverted ratios; missing ×100; "developed" as length not links; generic counter-argument without weighting. | Present in the 9609 Pack. |

## 04.7 Fading, worked examples and productive-failure openers

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-FADE-001 | Per-skill fading state machine, `skill_state ∈ {S1 worked_example, S2 completion_last_blank, S3 completion_first_given, S4 full_with_hints, S5 full_timed}`; advance after two consecutive correct at the current stage, regress one stage after two consecutive errors; initial stage from mastery (M<0.4 → S1; 0.4–0.7 → S3; >0.7 → S4). | State transitions unit-tested; stage stored per (student, skill). |
| 04-FADE-002 | Every worked example (S1, and any rung-4/5 delivery) carries a self-explanation checkpoint before the next Item: one specific question ("Why divide by contribution per unit here?"); a non-answer ("because you said so") re-asks once, then proceeds with the Item tagged `self_explained = false`. | 100% of worked examples followed by a checkpoint; field logged. |
| 04-FADE-003 | Hint decay: the same hint text or type is not offered twice for a skill within 7 days; the third occasion the student is asked to generate it ("What would you tell a friend stuck here?"). | Hint hash log per skill. |
| 04-FADE-004 | **Pre-test opener.** The first Session on any syllabus point begins with a 3–5 Item probe with confidence ratings, before any explanation; the probe uses ≥2 diagnostic Items (04.6) and one transfer Item; **feedback is given immediately on each probe Item**, without which the pretesting effect does not appear; the tutor's first explanatory Turn references the student's own wrong attempt ("You used price where contribution belongs — that is the whole idea of break-even."). This is pretesting / errorful generation — a small, reliable effect — and it is not productive failure (04-FADE-006). | 100% of first-topic Sessions begin with a probe; every probe Item returns feedback in the same Turn; the instruction Turn quotes the probe response. |
| 04-FADE-005 | For levels subjects the pre-test opener is a 5-minute "try before we teach" plan of a real-tariff question; the instruction that follows is organised around the AO the plan missed. | Same acceptance, levels variant. |
| 04-FADE-006 | **Productive-failure opener.** Phase A only (04.13), once per topic *cluster*, fired on `topic_cluster_first_session`: one complex, ill-structured contextual problem with **no** scaffolding and no hints, ≥8 minutes, in which the student is asked to generate as many different approaches as they can and is told that failing is the point. The tutor emits nothing but acknowledgements until the student stops or the timer ends; the instruction Turn that follows is organised around the gap between the student's own attempts and the canonical method and quotes at least two of them ("You tried dividing fixed costs by price, then by profit — both treat one unit as if it paid for everything"). | PF openers fire only when `phase = A` and `topic_cluster_first_session`; 0 hint or rung tokens emitted inside the PF window; the following instruction Turn quotes ≥2 of the student's attempts; PF and pre-test openers never fire in the same Session. |

Why: the d=0.36 productive-failure result is for multiple divergent attempts at *one complex problem* before instruction (04-FADE-006); a 3–5 Item probe is pretesting, whose gains are smaller and depend on immediate feedback (04-FADE-004). Keeping them separate stops the smaller effect from being claimed under the larger one's evidence. Self-explanation g=0.55.

## 04.9 Frustration, "I give up", off-topic and wellbeing signals

A classifier (Haiku-class, <300 ms) tags every student message with `affect ∈ {neutral, frustrated, give_up, off_topic, distress, crisis}`; the tag selects a response policy. Full scripts and the safety stack are in §12; the tutor-side rules are here.

| Signal | Detection | Tutor response |
|---|---|---|
| Frustration ("this is stupid", repeated "??", caps) | classifier + ≥2 failed attempts in 3 Turns | Drop one rung immediately (the only permitted descent, logged `rung_relief`), shorten to ≤60 words, name the concrete next step; no emotional commentary. |
| "I give up" / "just tell me" | classifier | Treat as "Just the answer" if the gate is satisfied at `gate_quality = substantive`; else offer the graded time-box (04-GATE-002) in one sentence with the chip. Never argue. |
| Off-topic (non-study) | classifier + no Course Item referenced | One sentence answer if harmless, then the placeholder returns to the Item; three consecutive off-topic turns → suggest an Ask outside the Course (does not count as study). |
| Distress ("I'm going to fail", 1 a.m. "I can't stop crying") | classifier | Warm, brief, honest, back to the plan. The sentence budget is owned by §12.5 and cited here, never restated: **≤4 sentences, of which one is the regional signpost when crisis language is present and three otherwise.** One concrete next action tied to the Plan ("Paper 2 is 41 days away; the next 25 minutes on break-even moves you most."), Study/Quiet Hours respected. No therapy-speak, no companion behaviour, no follow-up "how are you feeling". |
| Crisis language (self-harm, abuse) | classifier + keyword list (§12) | Hand off to the §12 safe-messaging flow immediately: signposting by region, tutor content suspended for the Session, school-tenant escalation per policy object. The tutor never continues the Item in the same Turn. |

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 04-WELL-001 | Every affect tag and the policy applied are logged on the Turn; distress and crisis counts feed the Sunday review's sleep line and never the parent digest as text. | Fields present; digest contains aggregates only (§07.9). |
| 04-WELL-002 | Between-papers mode: after a sat paper, requests to re-litigate it get one factual line ("That paper is done; the next one is Thursday.") and the next-paper Plan; no marking of the sat paper until after the last paper unless the student overrides in Settings. | Case `post_paper_spiral` passes. |

Why: condescension ("as if I am the panicked one") drives churn; the emotional load is real (12–15% use chatbots for support) and the product's answer is signposting plus a plan, not companionship.

## 04.11 The Cognitive-Debt Guard

The guard detects a student who is outsourcing the thinking and responds by restoring effort, never by punishing. MVP ships the six signals and L1 behind a user-level randomised flag; L2–L3 ship in v1 once the A/B has 28-day windows.

# §07 Learner memory and the knowledge graph

## 07.1 Entities and fields

## 07.2 Mastery: computation, states and decay

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 07-MAST-001 | `M_s` = retrievability-weighted mean over the point's Items of a per-Item correctness posterior: `M_s = Σ_i R_i · c_i / Σ_i R_i`, where `R_i` is current FSRS retrievability and `c_i` is a Beta(1,1)-prior posterior mean of correctness over the Item's Attempts, each Attempt weighted by rung penalty `w(rung) = {≤1: 1.0, 2: 0.8, 3: 0.6, 4: 0.3, 5: 0}` and by `0` if `seen_solution` or `intent = just_answer`. ## 07.5 Driving the Plan: Mark-Yield ranking

Mark-Yield is the number that decides what the student practises next. It is the expected qualification marks gained per minute of study on one syllabus point (or one skill node such as "AO4 committed judgement"), stored as `mastery[].yield` (07.1) and consumed by the Plan (§10.15), Today (§10.3) and the interleaved and new slots of every Session (04-MIX-003). It is SYNTHESIS §7.7's mark-yield written out with every variable sourced.

### Formula

`Y_s` is **expected weighted marks gained in the next fixed 20-minute block**, not a rate over the whole remaining effort. The distinction is the difference between a planner that invests and one that only tops up: dividing by total remaining effort lets a nearly-mastered point win because it is nearly finished.

```
Y_s = E_s × G_s × U_s × T_s × min(1, 20 / m_s) / 20    # weighted marks per minute over the next 20-minute block

E_s = Σ_p  w_p × P_p(s) × k_p(s)           # tariff exposure, in option-percentage points
G_s = max(0, M_target(s) − M_s)             # gain headroom
U_s = 1 − R_s(t_paper)                      # decay urgency
T_s = clamp(learnability_s × transfer_s, 0.5, 1.5)
m_s = max(5, base_min_s × √G_s × rung_factor_s + 8 × open_misconceptions_s)   # minutes to reach M_target
```

Two properties are load-bearing and are enforced by 07-YLD-007. First, `m_s` is **sublinear** in `G_s` (√, not linear): a point that needs twice the learning does not cost twice the minutes, so headroom does not cancel out of the ratio and `Y_s` stays strictly increasing in `G_s`. Second, the `min(1, 20/m_s)` factor caps the credit a short top-up can claim: a point five minutes from its target earns the marks it can earn in a 20-minute block, not a rate extrapolated from five minutes. SYNTHESIS §7.7's form (`tariff_exposure × (1 − M) × (1 − R) × learnability / est_hours`) is this with `M_target = 1` and `est_hours` independent of headroom; the sublinear `m_s` is what preserves SYNTHESIS's `(1 − M)` intent rather than cancelling it.

| Variable | Meaning | Source | Default / range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `w_p` | Value of one raw mark on paper *p* in the option grade = `papers[p].weight_pct / papers[p].max_marks` | Pack `papers[]` (§06.3) | 9609: P1 20/40 = 0.5, P2 30/60 = 0.5, P3 30/60 = 0.5, P4 20/40 = 0.5; differs on most syllabuses |
| `P_p(s)` | Probability *s* is assessed on paper *p* in the next series: Laplace-smoothed frequency over the last 8 series, `(hits + 1) / (series + 2)` | Pack `assessed_by` edges (07.6) built by the compiler from question metadata; skill nodes (AO3/AO4 techniques) have `P = 1.0` on every levels paper | 0.1–1.0 |
| `k_p(s)` | Mean raw marks tied to *s* on *p* when it appears | same edge, `marks_on_offer` | tariff-weighted |
| `M_target(s)` | Per-point mastery target, **exposure-weighted rather than flat**: `M_target(s) = clamp(M_grade + 0.15 × (2 × normalised(E_s) − 1), 0.55, 0.95)`, where `M_grade` is the Course `target_grade` → {A*: 0.90, A: 0.85, B: 0.75, C: 0.65, D/E: 0.55} and `normalised(E_s) = E_s / max_s E_s` within the Course. A flat target per grade tells the planner to leave every topic equally unfinished; real grade-C strategy is to secure the high-tariff, high-frequency basics completely and accept gaps on the rare ones. Floor 0.85 for skill nodes on levels papers. | Course `target_grade` × Pack exposure | 0.55–0.95 |
| `M_s` | Current mastery (07-MAST-001); cohort median until 30 Attempts (07-MAST-004); projected value if the point is `projected` from another board (07.6) | Learner Graph `mastery[].M` | 0–1 |
| `R_s(t_paper)` | FSRS retrievability of the point's Items projected to the date of the paper that assesses it, tariff-weighted mean; Unseen ⇒ 0 | Learner Graph `mastery[].retrievability_at_exam`, recomputed nightly (04-FSRS-002) | 0–1 |
| `learnability_s` | Cohort mastery gain per hour on *s* ÷ Course median gain per hour, for students at the same target grade | Cohort aggregates (07.8, k ≥ 20) | 1.0 until 200 cohort Attempts on *s*; clamp 0.6–1.4 |
| `transfer_s` | `1 + 0.25 × n_dep` where `n_dep` = number of points with a confirmed `prerequisite_of` edge from *s* that are below their own target, capped at 2; skill nodes take 1.5 | 07.6 graph | 1.0–1.5 |
| `base_min_s` | Cohort minutes from Unseen to `M = 1` on *s* at this grade | Pack `est_minutes` seeded by the compiler, replaced by cohort data at 200 Attempts | 30–120 |
| `rung_factor_s` | `1 + 0.1 × (rung_mean_30d − 1)` for this student on *s*; Course mean if no Attempts | Learner Graph `rung_mean_30d` | 1.0–1.4 |

The CourseTree badge (§10.4) shows expected marks recoverable, not the rate; the two are different numbers and are never swapped.

### Worked example — 9609 AS student, Paper 2 in 42 days (Phase B), target A

`M_grade = 0.85`; `max_s E_s = 9.0`, so `M_target` runs from 0.95 on the highest-exposure node to 0.75 on the lowest. Four candidates after the FSRS due slot is filled; syllabus numbering illustrative and read from the Pack (07-YLD-009).

| Skill: AO4 committed judgement (`P = 1.0` on P1 §B 6 AO4 marks and P2 12 AO4 marks; `w` 0.5) | 0.5×1.0×6 + 0.5×1.0×12 = **9.0** | 0.95 | 0.95 − 0.30 = 0.65 | 1 − 0.50 = 0.50 | 0.8 × 1.5 = 1.2 | 60×√0.65×1.2 = **58.0** | 0.3445 | 9.0×0.65×0.50×1.2×0.3445/20 = **0.0605** | **≈ +7.3 marks/h · Paper 2** |
| 5.1.2 Motivation theories — Secure, decaying (P2 0.7×12; P1 §B 0.4×20) | 4.2 + 4.0 = **8.2** | 0.95 | 0.95 − 0.72 = 0.23 | 1 − 0.55 = 0.45 | 1.0 | 60×√0.23×1.0 = **28.8** | 0.6951 | **0.0295** | ≈ +3.5 marks/h |

Read the ranking honestly rather than as a slogan. The AO4 skill leads because it is assessed on every essay on both papers — nine exposure points against break-even's three — and because the student is at 0.30 on it. Motivation still edges out break-even on exposure alone (8.2 against 3.15), not on being nearly finished: its headroom of 0.23 buys it a `min(1, 20/m_s)` of 0.70 where break-even's 0.465 buys 0.35, and the two effects nearly cancel. The gap between the two, 1.3×, was 1.9× under a formula in which `m_s` was linear in `G_s` and headroom cancelled out of the ratio altogether. Lean production, unseen, ranks last on this list and is nonetheless scheduled before T−21 by the coverage rule (07-YLD-008) — the ranking decides order, not whether a syllabus point is ever taught. Why: the Layer-1 RCT `mark-yield vs weakest-topic` (13-EFF-003) is only meaningful if the ranking is a swappable strategy behind one interface — build `rank(strategy, course, user) → [(point, score, why)]`.

## 07.6 The cross-board syllabus graph

The cross-board graph is the layer that lets one explanation, one misconception bank and one original Item serve 9609, Edexcel IAL Business, IB Business Management and, where content overlaps, a national exam — while keeping everything an examiner owns (schemes, grids, thresholds) strictly per board. It lives in two Postgres tables, `syllabus_nodes` and `syllabus_edges`; nothing here is a graph database (§11).

### Schema

```yaml
syllabus_node:
  node_id: "9609:2026-2028/5.1.2"        # pack_id + official numbering; skill nodes "9609:2026-2028/skill/ao4_judgement"
  pack_id, kind: content|skill|command_word|paper
  title: "Motivation theories: Taylor, Maslow, Herzberg"   # our wording
  level: IGCSE|GCSE|AS|A2|IAL_AS|IAL_A2|IB_SL|IB_HL|national
  ao_profile: {AO1: 0.3, AO2: 0.2, AO3: 0.3, AO4: 0.2}     # from assessed_by edges
  misconception_ids[], item_family_ids[], embedding_id      # voyage-4, 1024-d

syllabus_edge:
  edge_id, from_node, to_node
  type: equivalent_to|prerequisite_of|part_of|assessed_by
  # equivalent_to only:
  coverage: full|partial_from_in_to|partial_to_in_from|overlap
  depth_delta: -1|0|+1                     # +1 = to_node goes deeper (e.g. IB HL extensions)
  vocabulary_map: {"gearing": "leverage", "trade receivables": "debtors"}   # optional, per direction
  # assessed_by only:
  paper_id, weight_pct, frequency_8_series: 5, marks_on_offer_mean: 8.0    # feeds P_p(s), k_p(s) in 07.5
  confidence: 0.0–1.0
  status: proposed|confirmed|rejected
  provenance:
    method: compiler_embedding|compiler_llm|human_confirmed|human_authored
    models: ["sonnet-5@2026-08", "gemini-3.8-flash"]   # cross-family for compiler_llm
    evidence: [{pack_id, source_ref, extract≤40w}]      # both syllabus lines
    reviewer_id?, reviewed_at?, review_note?
  created_at, superseded_by?
```

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 07-XBRD-001 | `equivalent_to` is stored once and read symmetrically; `coverage` and `vocabulary_map` are direction-aware. `part_of` edges form the Pack's tree and are the only edges the compiler may auto-confirm (≥0.95, two families agree). `equivalent_to` and `prerequisite_of` are never auto-confirmed. | Schema constraint + compiler unit test. |
| 07-XBRD-002 | **Proposal pipeline** (a stage of the Curriculum Compiler, §06.4): (1) embed every node's title + our description + misconception labels; (2) candidate pairs across Packs at cosine ≥0.78 (top-5 per node); (3) Sonnet 5 batch classifies each pair `{equivalent (coverage, depth_delta), prerequisite, none}` with a one-line justification quoting both syllabus lines; (4) Gemini 3.8 Flash repeats blind; (5) `confidence` = agreement-weighted mean of the two calibrated scores; (6) write `status=proposed`. Pairs <0.60 are dropped; 0.60–0.95 go to the mapping-review queue (§10.25). Cross-family disagreement on `type` forces review regardless of score. | Golden set of 200 human-labelled 9609↔IAL pairs: precision ≥0.90, recall ≥0.80 at the review threshold. |
| 07-XBRD-003 | **Human confirmation**: a subject reviewer confirms, edits (coverage, depth, vocabulary) or rejects; the UI shows both syllabus lines, both Packs' misconceptions and a sample Item from each; ≤20 s median per decision; confirmation writes `provenance.method=human_confirmed`. Rejected pairs are not re-proposed unless a Pack version changes. | Review throughput and audit trail; re-proposal test. |
| 07-XBRD-004 | Edges are versioned with the Packs they join; a new syllabus version re-runs proposals only for changed nodes and carries confirmed edges forward when both node texts are unchanged (sha256 of our description). | Re-run touches only changed nodes. |

### What transfers, what never does

| Asset | Transfers across a confirmed `equivalent_to`? | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Concept explanations, worked examples, contrast cases (Pack `content`) | Yes | Served in the target Course with `vocabulary_map` applied and the target board's command-word table; the citation chip names the *target* syllabus line. `depth_delta = +1` withholds the deeper material's extension until the target Pack has its own node text. |
| Misconceptions and distractors | Yes | Copied as `suspected` priors at weight 0.5 in the target Course's misconception list; `confirmed` requires evidence on the target Course (04-MISC-004). | Cards and FSRS state | Yes, for atomic Items with an `item_alias` (definitions, formulas, diagram rules) | The Card keeps its provenance line ("From your Mark · 9609 P2 Q3(b)") and gains the target Course; stability, difficulty and `due` carry over unchanged; the interval cap re-anchors to the target paper (04-FSRS-003). | Original Items (OWN) | Conditionally | Retagged to the target node only if `command_word` exists verbatim in the target Pack and the tariff/AO split is legal for a target paper (05-GEN-001); otherwise the generator is called with the target paper's constraints. `item_stats` are kept per board, never pooled. |
| Mark schemes, levels grids, AO splits, evidence rules | **Never** | Board property; a Mark is always produced from the target Pack's `mark_scheme_items`. |
| Command-word definitions and AO ceilings | **Never** | Verbatim per syllabus version (05-PROC-012). |
| Thresholds, PUM tables, predicted grades | **Never** | Per component per series (05.8). |
| Examiner insights | **Never** as evidence | May be shown as background in the source Course only; a Mark on the target Course cites only the target board's insights. |
| Marks, Attempts, Receipts | **Never moved** | They stay on the Course that produced them; the target Course sees their effect only through projected mastery. |
| Calibration status | **Never** | Each Examiner Twin is per paper (05-SCOP-003). |
