# Product Decisions — the synthesis the master prompt is built on

## 0. How to read this / status of decisions (final vs provisional vs founder-owned)
This is the single source of truth for the seven section writers (W1–W7) and the editor. It merges three product visions (**P** = vision-pedagogy, **C** = vision-craft, **F** = vision-platform) with the research index (`research/INDEX.md`: decisions **[A1–A61]**, numbers **[B]**, contradictions **[C1–C14]**, founder questions **[E1–E17]**) and the two gap briefs (**GM** marking/Cambridge, **GL** legal/incumbents). Every ruling below was made in `designs/JUDGING.md`; writers do not re-open them.

**Status labels.** Every decision here carries one of three statuses; if a paragraph carries none, it is FINAL.
- **FINAL** — write it into the master prompt as a requirement.
- **PROVISIONAL** — the default the master prompt uses; the label says what evidence or event would flip it (an A/B result, a licence, a measured human–human line). Writers include the flip condition.
- **FOUNDER-OWNED** — listed in §15 with the recommended default. Writers use the default and mark the paragraph "[founder default]" so the founder can find every dependent sentence.

**How to cite.** Keep the bracket references in drafts; the editor strips them only where they would confuse an executing agent. INDEX §B numbers with confidence **L** are our own decisions — phrase them as targets.

**Non-negotiables** (the executing agent must never trade these away; they appear in master-prompt §00): the effort gate [A9]; no marks without a mark scheme in context and none shown below gate [A18][A22]; the no-sycophancy lint [A13]; no daily streaks or shame mechanics [A40]; no submittable coursework [A59]; the three-provenance content model (§9); teen-safety parity [A58]; no efficacy claim that is not pre-registered [A7]; never "replaces teachers" [A5].

**Terminology is fixed.** Course, Pack, Item, Attempt, Turn, Mark, Card, Session, Plan, Source, Receipt, Class (§3), capitalised everywhere. Modes: **Learn · Practise · Mark** (the four-mode vocabulary in [A35] is retired). Intent chips: **Teach me · Check my steps · Just the answer**. Mastery: **Unseen · Shaky · Secure · Exam-ready**.

## 1. Product definition and working name
**Working name: Margin** — PROVISIONAL until UKIPO/EUIPO/USPTO/WIPO class 9/41/42 searches and domain checks (margin.study, usemargin.com, margin.app) clear; fallback **Nib**. Chosen over the other eight candidates (Bestfit, Kalibre, Retrieva, Quire, Marksmith, Calibra, SecondMarker) because it carries both halves of the promise in one plain word — the margin is where an examiner writes, and "6 marks to an A" is a margin — it reads calm rather than clever, contains no board's mark [E10], and does not acquire an unwanted meaning in Russian or Kazakh (Kalibre/Calibra read as gun calibre; Marksmith transliterates as "Marx-smith"). Never pair the name with "Cambridge"; a non-affiliation notice ships in footer and store listing (GL§A5).

**One-sentence definition.** Margin is an AI study workspace that marks like an examiner and teaches like a tutor: it grounds every explanation in the student's own syllabus, mark schemes and examiner reports, marks work per Assessment Objective with quoted evidence and a published agreement number, and schedules what to learn next so that more marks are earned per hour studied — for Cambridge International first, and for every curriculum after.

**Tagline (FINAL for the master prompt; marketing may test variants):** *"They know everything. We know your exam."* [INDEX G01]. Secondary line for the Mark surface: *"Marked like an examiner. Explained like a tutor."*

**What it is not** (write these into §01 of the master prompt as honest limits): not smarter than the frontier models it runs on; not a past-paper mirror; not an answer service [A4]; not a replacement for teachers or tutors [A5]; not endorsed by any board; not "2× learning" [A7].

**Category sentence:** an exam-outcome company built as a data platform (F§1) — calibration data, structured mark schemes, the learner graph and school trust — with a chat interface on top that matches a frontier chat [A8].

## 2. Positioning: the 10 differentiators
Final wording, ordered by defensibility (how hard for a lab to copy with a prompt), each with its evidence hook and the competitor it beats. W1 uses these ten verbatim as the spine of master-prompt §01 and may add colour, not claims.

1. **A marker as consistent as a second examiner — with the number published per paper.** *Hook:* students' #1 pain is lenient, drifting AI marking ("ChatGPT predicts me like 16 marks and in reality I'll get 3") [A3]; frontier models already sit at or above the examiner–examiner line on 32,534 double-marked real GCSE scripts (maths QWK 0.84) but are more lenient than trained examiners and vary run-to-run (GM§0). We add what a prompt cannot: gold sets from Example Candidate Responses and teacher double-marking, board-style seeding with auto-pause, dual-family ensembles and a public calibration page per paper [A22][A23]. *Beats:* every lab study mode and every "AI marking" incumbent (Save My Exams, Medly, Seneca, Paperstack) — none publishes accuracy (GL§B3).
2. **Marks per Assessment Objective with quoted spans, following the board's own procedure.** *Hook:* Cambridge best-fit per AO grid, "developed analysis = two or more links in the chain", the cover-the-business-name test [A19][GM§B1.2]; a credited point without a quoted span is a bug. *Beats:* ChatGPT's holistic mark from imagination; SME's short-answer-only marking.
3. **Feedback in the examiner's own language, highest-yield lost mark first.** *Hook:* feedback works through information content (d=0.48); person-praise is among the weakest forms [A13]; examiner reports repeat the same criticisms for years [GM§B1.6]. *Beats:* every chat product (sycophancy is students' second-most-hated trait [SV#0]).
4. **A Course, not a chat.** *Hook:* no lab product maps an answer to a syllabus code, AO, command word or mark-scheme line; Study Mode cannot run inside ChatGPT Projects [A2]. The Course carries the Pack; mode is a visible chip; there is no study toggle to lose [A35]. *Beats:* the lab study modes ("So they've removed study mode?").
5. **The tutor prepared for the lesson.** *Hook:* every positive AI-tutoring RCT gave the tutor a verified key; critics call Study Mode "a tutor that didn't prep for the session" [A18]. We pre-solve every Item with a strong model and SymPy and never grade without the scheme in context [A51]. *Beats:* chat that "makes up chemicals" [SV#2.1].
6. **An effort gate that produces exam gains, not practice inflation — and never a refusal.** *Hook:* Bastani (n≈1,000): unguarded GPT +48% practice, worse exam; guarded tutor +127%, no harm [A9]; off-the-shelf models leak the answer in the opening turn >96% of the time [A17]. Enforced by an orchestrator vetting every draft, "Just the answer" honoured after one attempt (labelled, retested), leak rate a CI gate <5%. *Beats:* study modes that concede they "may give a direct answer" [A1] and Socratic theatre that "annoys you into learning" [A11].
7. **A learner model that persists and decays honestly.** *Hook:* ChatGPT degrades after ~40–50 messages and forgets between sessions [A48]; students keep spreadsheets of specification points [SV wish 7]. Mastery, misconceptions, seen-solution items and calibration live outside the chat on an FSRS-6 clock anchored to the exam date [A52]. *Beats:* memory that remembers what you said, not what you know.
8. **A predicted grade computed the way the board computes it.** *Hook:* raw → component threshold → PUM → option grade, a range across recent series with "marks to next grade", A* only at option level [A24]. *Beats:* chat products that hallucinate boundaries; notes sites with no grade model; Up Learn's opaque guarantee.
9. **A compliant content model schools can endorse.** *Hook:* Cambridge refuses electronic publication of papers, schemes and reports; every CAIE "papers + AI" app runs on tolerance (GL§0). OWN / LICENSED / USER provenance, deep links, transient bring-your-own-paper, original items, published takedown SLA [A33]. *Beats:* tools no school can officially recommend.
10. **Calm, fast, honest, teen-safe by design.** *Hook:* "walls of text" and limit opacity are top complaints [A54]; introjected motivation predicts ill-being [A40]; only ChatGPT has a real teen product, US-first [A58]. ≤120-word Turns, TTFT p50 <800 ms, exact-page citations <400 ms, published limits with exam-week grace, no streaks, no ads, parental controls without transcripts. *Beats:* chat built for everyone, not for a 17-year-old with Paper 2 in 41 days (C§1).

**Honesty clause:** never "replaces teachers" [A5]; never an efficacy multiplier [A7]; never "official" or "endorsed"; the marketing number equals the product number.

## 3. Product model: the core objects, the loops, the first 10 minutes, first week, exam week
### 3.1 Core objects (FINAL vocabulary; data model in §11)
- **Course** — the home object: `{board, syllabus_code, syllabus_version, level, exam_session, target_grade, papers[]}`, e.g. "Cambridge A Level Business 9609 (2026–28) · June 2027 · target A" [A2]. Owns its Pack version, syllabus tree with mastery states, Attempts, Cards, Plan, memory, files and Sessions. No "New chat" on Home; a conversation without a Course is an **Ask** and never counts as study.
- **Pack** — the curriculum data for one syllabus version: papers with AO weights, verbatim command words with AO ceilings, numbered content tree with misconceptions and distractors, questions (metadata; text only where OWN/LICENSED), structured mark-scheme items, examiner insights, threshold history, exemplars, links (F§7.1). Readiness `draft → ingested → mapped → gold-seeded → gated → live`. A new board is a new Pack, never new code [A29].
- **Item** — any atomic retrievable or gradable unit (definition, formula, skill, evaluation point, diagram rule, or an exam-style **Question part** with tariff, command word, AO split and scheme), with `provenance ∈ {OWN, LICENSED, USER}` and stats `(n, p_correct, discrimination, mean_latency)`.
- **Attempt** — the atomic record of learning: `{item_id, syllabus_points[], response, transcript_confirmed, mark_per_AO, rung_reached, confidence, latency_to_first_token, seen_solution, policy_version, teacher_override, timestamp}`. Every metric, mastery update, teacher view and Receipt is a projection of Attempts. **Scoping rule: a feature that does not create or improve Attempts is not in the MVP** (P§3).
- **Turn** — one Learn-mode assistant message: ≤120 words, one move, one question, explicit hand-back [A12]; carries `rung, lint_result, model, cache_hit`.
- **Mark** — the per-AO marking record for an Attempt (contract in §8.1).
- **Card** — an FSRS-6 retrieval item with provenance ("From your Mark · 9609 P2 Q3(b)") [A52].
- **Session** — a conversation scoped to one Course and one topic by default; drifting prompts a new Session [A48].
- **Plan** — weekly blocks on an exam spine from the phase mix and mark-yield priorities (§7.7); proposed, never silently applied.
- **Source** — a page-addressable document with `trust_rank` (syllabus 1 > endorsed textbook 2 > mark scheme 3 > examiner report 4 > revision notes 5 > user upload 6) and provenance class [A31].
- **Receipt** — the signed exportable process log per assignment [A59]. **Class** — a teacher's group bound to one Course [A55].

### 3.2 The loops (FINAL)
**Session (12–25 min weekdays, 25–45 weekends).** Scheduler-generated: due retrieval with confidence ratings → **one marked exam-style Item at real tariff** (2–5 marks on weekdays; 8–12 marks once or twice a week; a 20-marker weekly from 8 weeks out) → the lost mark becomes a Card → one ladder climb on the gap → a 20-second predict–postdict → a three-line end card ("18 cards · 1 question marked · 3.2 Motivation moved to ¾"). No streak, no confetti [A40]. Mix per §7.7.
**Day.** Home shows Continue · Due · Next [A43]; ≤1 opt-in notification; Study/Quiet Hours respected [A58].
**Week (Sunday, 10 min, "Readiness review").** Points that changed state; the calibration curve ("you said 'sure' 40 times; 26 were right"); next week's mark-yield list; one timed section; the sleep-aware Plan; a weekly learning-event goal with two rest days, hidden in exam weeks.
**Exam cycle.** September: cold-open diagnostic probes seed mastery [A14]. Oct–Nov: first mock imported → per-question post-mortem → weak-topic map. January: mock results met with a plan, not platitudes. Easter: past-paper blitz by mark-yield; retention 0.90 at 3–8 weeks, 0.95 inside 3 [A52]. **Between-papers mode** from T−7 per component: next paper only, no re-litigating, no new material unless overridden, no blocks beyond 90 min after 22:00 (month-before sleep ≈25% of grade variance) [A60], grace-mode limits, distress script (§7.10). Results day: consented actual grade → predicted-vs-actual feeds calibration [A61].

### 3.3 The first 10 minutes (FINAL; C's Mark-first path is the default landing)
**0:00** One composer, one line ("Paste an answer, or photograph one. We'll mark it like an examiner."), four sample chips ("9609 12-mark evaluation" · "0580 vectors" · "9701 mechanism" · "Photograph your own"); no hero, no account. **0:20** Paste or photograph; a two-field sheet asks board and subject; photos show the transcript for confirmation [A25]. **0:40** The Mark streams: header, one AO card each within 8 s, full ≤45 s, credited spans lighting up in the answer; a band not a point; "real examiners disagree by ±N on items like this"; calibration badge or "practice estimate — not yet calibrated"; a citation chip to the scheme line. **1:30** The chip opens the right pane at the exact line or attributed extract [A36][A33]. **2:00** One primary action: "Rewrite the weakest paragraph — one link in the chain gets you to L2." **4:00** Re-mark; the delta fades in ("+3 · AO4 L1→L2"). **5:00** A 3-item retrieval probe with confidence ratings, no praise [A14]. **6:00** The effort gate on a fresh Item with the three intent chips; the deal stated once: "I'll mark like an examiner, I won't hand you answers before you've tried, and you can always ask for one." **8:00** "Save this Course" → level, session, exam date → age band and consent [A58] → optional import (mock photo, Anki deck, timetable). **10:00** Course home: syllabus tree with pips, readiness ring, "Next exam in 243 days", first Today plan. Targets in §5 bet 2. Class-code and app-store entry use board → subjects → date sheets instead, still ending in a marked Item before account creation.

### 3.4 First week and exam week (FINAL)
**First week.** Day 1–2: diagnostic probes on what school is teaching now (productive failure before instruction) [A14]; Course memory visible and editable. Day 2–3: first ladder session; first "5 more like this"; first labelled "Just the answer" with its near-transfer item [A11]. Day 3: first seen-solution retest. Day 4: first handwriting Mark. Day 5: a timed 30-minute section. Day 7: first Readiness review and calibration curve; first Secure pip; invitation to add a class code or parent; ≥30 Attempts so the planner drops cohort priors.
**Exam week** = between-papers mode (§3.2) plus: Home collapses to one paper ("Paper 2 · Thursday 09:00 · 3 days"); 25-minute sessions; default intent Check my steps; entry rung rises (no worked examples at Secure+) [A10]; no rings, deltas or predicted grades; ≤1 factual notification/day; sat papers archive silently.

## 5. Unique bets (final 8–10) with success measures
Ten bets, each with what it is, why it works, and how we would know. Every bet names the metric it moves [A61].

1. **The Examiner Twin with a published number** (P bet 1, F bet 1). One calibrated marker per *paper*, standardised on ECRs and teacher double-marked scripts, seeded in production like a board, ensembled across two model families, with challenge-a-mark and a public calibration page ("Calibrated on 9609 P2 · agreement 0.82 · 312 scripts"). *Why:* frontier models are already near the second-examiner line; the missing ingredient is board-style QA (GM§0). *Know:* per-paper QWK ≥ human line and floor; run-to-run ≤1; challenge <3%, upheld <30%; the marketing number equals the product number.
2. **The Mark before the account** (C bet 1). An anonymous visitor gets a per-AO Mark with quoted evidence in 90 seconds; onboarding happens after. *Why:* marking is the #1 pain and the first Mark is the "aha" nobody else can deliver [A3]. *Know:* landing → full Mark ≥55%; Mark → saved Course ≥35%; median time-to-first-Mark ≤90 s.
3. **Answer Anatomy — mark schemes as data** (P bet 3, F bet 2). Marking points with conjunctive conditions, levels grids per AO with evidence rules, indicative content, ER insights and misconceptions as first-class rows, editable via paste → structured in <2 minutes per part. *Why:* the founder's most valuable file is exactly this derived layer; it turns an LLM into an examiner and is the legally defensible asset [A32][CS§3]. *Know:* per-marking-point F1 ≥0.9; ≥95% of feedback lines cite a specific point or descriptor.
4. **Mark-yield planning** (P bet 2). Syllabus points ranked by expected marks recoverable per hour, not "weakest topic". *Why:* effort belongs where retrieval is about to fail on heavily assessed material; students cannot compute this from a spreadsheet [SV wish 7]. *Know:* predicted-grade movement per hour higher than a weakest-topic arm (in-product RCT).
5. **Exam-anchored memory** (P bet 6). FSRS-6 per Item, retention 0.85 → 0.90 → 0.95, intervals capped at the paper date, Cepeda prior, timed papers replacing Cards inside three weeks. *Why:* the retention interval is *known* — the exam date [LS§1.3]. *Know:* 7/28-day unaided recall ≥85%/≥80%; per-user log loss ≤0.35.
6. **Calibration as an outcome** (P bet 4). Confidence on every gradable Attempt, predict–postdict on every mock, a weekly curve, overconfident-wrong items at ×0.5 interval with self-explanation. *Why:* monitoring accuracy is trainable (g≈0.57) [A14]. *Know:* Brier falls per student over 8 weeks; the predict–postdict gap narrows.
7. **The cognitive-debt guard with a visible dividend** (P bet 5). Six signals, graduated responses, always paired with the student's own unaided-score delta; randomised. *Know:* 28-day retention and mock marks; ≤2 pp 30-day retention cost.
8. **The Learning Receipt** (P bet 7). A signed process log per assignment plus a hard refusal to write submittable coursework. *Why:* 64% of US high-schoolers fear false accusation [A59]. *Know:* teacher endorsement rate; zero substantiated coursework cases.
9. **The compliant three-provenance content model** (F bet 5). OWN / LICENSED / USER enforced at retrieval, deep links, provenance ledger, published takedown SLA, no Cambridge mark-scheme viewer. *Why:* schools cannot recommend tolerance-based tools; the compliant player wins procurement and the Cambridge conversation (GL§B4). *Know:* pilots won on compliance; zero takedowns; licences signed.
10. **The curriculum compiler and cross-board graph** (F bets 3–4). PDFs → gated Pack in <4 weeks with `equivalent_to` edges so one explanation bank serves sibling curricula. *Why:* packs-as-data is the only way to scale sub-linearly with boards. *Know:* weeks per Pack falling; >60% of a sibling pack covered by mapped content.


## 6. Information architecture and canonical screen list
**Top-level IA (FINAL):** Home · Course (Learn / Papers / Cards / Plan / Progress) · Library · Search (⌘K) · Settings, plus Teacher, Parent and internal Admin/Compiler. Mobile is a back-arrow route stack (C§7.1).

**Layout rulings.** Desktop ≥1280 px: grid `280px 1fr 480px` (sidebar 240–360, right pane 360–640, persisted) [A34]. **Right-pane rail (FINAL, JUDGING 8):** the default state is a 48 px rail with a citation-count badge; it opens on the first citation chip, artifact or Mark; Mark view and Reader open it by default; the centre column never exceeds 68 ch. ## 7. Pedagogy engine decisions
Owner: W3 (master-prompt §04, §07). All FINAL unless marked.

**7.1 Turn anatomy.** Every Learn-mode Turn ≤120 words unless the student asked for depth, in fixed order: (1) what was right — task-level, specific, unadorned; (2) one diagnosis, named with the misconception label; (3) one move — a question *or* a hint *or* a micro-example; (4) the hand-back, which is behaviour not text: the composer takes focus and its placeholder names the action ("Try the next line") [A12]. Marking cards are exempt from the word budget. The orchestrator rejects a draft that asks the student more than one question, solves beyond the current step, restates the whole problem, or contains person-praise, exclamation marks, emoji or "Great question" [A13][A17]; a rejected draft regenerates once with the violation named, then falls back to a template turn; lint runs before paint.

**7.2 Help ladder and entry rung.** Rungs: 1 metacognitive ("which concept family?") · 2 conceptual pointer (principle, no numbers) · 3 procedural (next step only) · 4 partial worked step with a blank · 5 full solution followed immediately by a near-transfer item done unaided [A10]. One rung per turn; the ladder resets per Item; rung reached is stored on the Attempt and shown to teachers only in aggregate [C5]. **Entry rung = f(mastery, item type):** M<0.4 → rung 4; 0.4–0.7 → rung 2; M>0.7 → rung 1, with rung 4 refused until two failed attempts (expertise reversal d=+0.505/−0.428). **Points/STEM:** rung 4 is a partial worked step, rung 5 a full verified solution. **Levels/essay:** rung 4 is a model *paragraph* one level above the student's current AO level with the evaluative sentence blanked, never a model essay; rung 5 on a 20-marker is a plan (define → two context-linked chains → counter → weighted judgement) plus one exemplar paragraph; full model answers exist only for past papers and only after the student's own marked Attempt (JUDGING 5).

**7.3 Effort gate.** No solution content on a gradable Item before an attempt, a plan, or "don't know where to start" plus one sentence of what they do know [A9]. The gate is a state on the Attempt, shown as a "Your go first" chip with a 90-second time-box option. Never a refusal in prose; never rate-limited [C5].

**7.4 Intent handling.** Reply row under the tutor Turn on gradable Items: **Teach me** (ladder) · **Check my steps** (student's answer first, then confirmation with reasoning) · **Just the answer** (enabled after one attempt or the one-sentence "don't know"; before that present with the tooltip "After one attempt") [A11]. The answer script is fixed: name the deal once ("Here it is — and one like it right after so it sticks"), deliver rung 5, tag the Item **Seen solution · retest Thu** (never counts toward Secure), schedule the retest at 24–72 h. Never lecture, never deny withholding if asked. Homework-due-in-10-minutes is legitimate: answer, then schedule an unaided redo tomorrow.

**7.5 Misconception diagnosis.** Every syllabus point carries ≥3 misconceptions mined from examiner reports with diagnostic distractors (9609: revenue vs profit, cash flow vs profit, business name as "application", evaluation inside Analyse items) [A15][GM§B1.6]. On a wrong answer: ask for the reasoning trace, classify **slip / misconception / missing prerequisite**, route: slip → quick fix; misconception → contrast case (two items differing in one feature); prerequisite → detour to the prerequisite node, which is then scheduled. The classification feeds the student's misconception prior.

**7.6 Fading.** Per skill: worked example → completion (last step blank) → completion (first step given) → full item with hints → full item, timed; advance after two consecutive correct, regress after two errors; a self-explanation checkpoint gates every worked example [LS§3.4].

**7.7 FSRS, exam-anchored retention and the phase-dependent mix.** FSRS-6 per atomic Item; desired retention 0.85 (>8 weeks to the paper), 0.90 (3–8), 0.95 (<3); intervals capped at the paper date; Cepeda prior (first review at 10–20% of time-to-exam) for new material inside 8 weeks; shrinkage fit from ~400 reviews, full parameters from ~1,000 [A52][C8]; overconfident-wrong items ×0.5 interval; two-button grading [A39]. **Mix by phase (FINAL, JUDGING 2):** Phase A (>8 weeks) ≥60% due retrieval / ≥25% interleaved practice from the last three topics / ≤15% new; Phase B (3–8 weeks) ≥50 / ≥35 including one timed section per week / ≤15; Phase C (<3 weeks) ≥50% timed sections or papers plus debrief-derived retrieval, ≤40% due Cards restricted to exam-critical Items, 0% new unless overridden; between-papers mode from T−7 per component. No blocked run >5 same-type items after first exposure. Mark-yield priority fills the "new" and "interleaved" slots: `yield_s = tariff_exposure_s × (1 − M_s) × (1 − R_s(t_exam)) × learnability_s / est_hours_s`, cohort medians until ≥30 Attempts.

**7.8 Mastery states.** M_s ∈ [0,1] = retrievability-weighted mean of a correctness posterior over the point's Items, penalised by rung depth (a rung-5 correct contributes 0). **Unseen** (no Attempt) · **Shaky** (attempted, or Secure with retrievability <0.7) · **Secure** (two unaided correct across ≥2 days at tariff, no seen-solution credit) · **Exam-ready** (Secure + one correct under timed conditions at full tariff with calibrated confidence). Pips fade rather than lie; every state carries a "why".

**7.9 Calibration loops.** Confidence (Sure · Think so · Guessing) on every gradable Attempt; Brier per topic and overall, weekly; predict–postdict–compare on every mock and the daily marked item; the tutor comments on the gap, not the score [A14].

**7.10 Cognitive-debt guard, coursework, wellbeing.** Signals over a session and rolling 14 days: solution-request ratio, attempt latency <8 s, copy-paste of tutor text, re-explanation without attempt, widening confidence–accuracy gap, output-creation vs practice ratio [A16]. Responses: L1 next session opens with three unaided items on yesterday's seen-solution items (MVP); L2 entry rung +1 for 48 h; L3 brain-first mode (tutor read-only until an attempt) (v1) — always paired with the student's own unaided-score delta; user-level randomised; kill-switch at −2 pp 30-day retention. **Coursework:** NEA/IA/EE requests switch to draft-feedback mode; never produce submittable text [A59]. **Distress:** warm, brief, honest, back to the plan, crisis signposting; never companion behaviour [A60][A58].

**7.11 Feedback template and no-sycophancy lint.** Feedback = task → process → self-regulation, opening with the highest-yield lost mark, in ER language with the ER cited [A13]. Banned: person-praise, unearned praise, agreement with an incorrect claim, softening a mark, exclamation marks, emoji, "Great question", therapy-speak. Weekly praise audit on 1% of Turns (2% in essay subjects); target <5% [LS§3.6].

**7.12 Learning Receipt.** Per assignment: attempts, hints, rungs, drafts, Marks, timestamps, seen-solution events, signed hash; PDF export and stripped public link [A59].

**7.13 Enforcement.** Cached system instructions plus the vetting orchestrator; every prompt or model change passes 30-turn adversarial simulated-student evals (leak <5%, SafeTutors dimensions, subject profiles) [A17][LS§3.12]. The tutor pre-solves every Item with a strong model plus SymPy and holds the key [A18][A51].

## 8. Exam engine decisions
Owner: W4 (master-prompt §05). All FINAL unless marked.

**8.1 Marking output contract (per question part).** `header {question_id, tariff, command_word, ao_split_for_this_paper} → total_band {low, modal, high} → per_AO [{ao, level, marks, descriptor_relied_on, credited_spans[{quote, line_ref, marking_point_id}], partial_spans[], uncredited_attempts[{quote, reason}]}] → command_word_check {ceiling_ao, met, coaching_line} → next_mark_advice (ER language, ER citation) → confidence_per_AO → calibration_status {gated|provisional|uncalibrated, qwk, n_scripts, last_audit} → sources[] → provenance {model_family, policy_version, second_marker?} → challenge_state`. A credited point without a quoted span fails the eval. Streaming: header first, one AO card each within 8 s, full ≤45 s [B].

**8.2 Structured mark-scheme schema** (F§7.1, GM§B4a, CS§5). `mark_scheme_items {id, question_id, scheme_type ∈ {points, levels, mixed}, marking_points [{id:"M1", type ∈ {M,A,B,DM,DB,FT}, credit_text, conjunctive_conditions[], all_required, depends_on, ecf_allowed, cao, isw, accept[], reject[], ao}], levels [{ao, max, bands [{level, range, descriptor, evidence_rule}]}], best_fit, ecf_policy, indicative_content[], generic_principles_ref, wording_status ∈ {own_paraphrase, licensed_verbatim}}`. Conjunctive conditions ("lone pair AND curly arrow from lone pair to carbonyl C") are split into checkable atoms; "//" alternatives credited once; "ORA" flagged. `examiner_insights {question_id | syllabus_node_id, series, pattern, text (our paraphrase), source_citation, heuristic}` carry machine-checkable heuristics.

**8.3 Two engines, board procedures.** *Points engine:* M/A/B/DM/DB/FT types, conjunctive conditions, accept/reject lists, **ECF** from the candidate's own prior values within scope and across parts where allowed, ISW, CAO, units and sig-fig penalties once per paper, SymPy recompute of every step; annotations ✓ × Ø BOD CON RE GA ECF NC I [A20]. *Levels engine:* each AO levelled independently from the full grid with **all levels defined** (accuracy falls as levels are omitted, GM§0), best-fit within an AO only, "developed" = ≥2 counted causal links, application credited only for facts true of *this* business (cover-the-name test), evaluation-in-Analyse scored 0 with the reason [A19][GM§B1.2]. Procedure prompts selected from `boards.level_procedure`: Cambridge best-fit; AQA ladder-up; OCR top-down; Pearson bullet-level evidence.

**8.4 Command-word check.** Before marking, match the command word to its AO ceiling from the Pack's verbatim table (Define/State → AO1; Explain/Calculate → AO1–2; Analyse → AO1–3; Discuss/Evaluate/Assess/Recommend/Justify → AO1–4); a mismatch caps the level and yields one coaching line [A21]. Definitions come verbatim from each syllabus, never from memory.

**8.5 Calibration method and gates — the final rule.** *Gold sets:* Tier 0 = Example Candidate Responses and specimen answers; Tier 1 = partner-school scripts double-marked by two teachers with the official scheme, both marks kept (this yields the human–human line per question family); Tier 2 = confirmed teacher overrides; ~300 scripts per paper, stratified by tariff, command word, handwritten/typed, length (GM§A4.1) [E6]. *Method:* context always includes the grid, generic principles, command-word definition, indicative content and 3–5 scored anchors; marker *instructions* (never the grid) refined Reflect-and-Revise style against ~200 held-out human-marked scripts per question family, then versioned; temperature 0, structured JSON, n=3 self-consistency on ≥12-mark items; examiner persona and negative exemplars against leniency [A23][GM§0]. **Ship gate per paper (FINAL, JUDGING 1):** marking becomes visible when the paper meets **both** (a) the absolute floor — points exact ≥97%, ±1 ≥99.5%, per-mark-point F1 ≥0.9; 8–12-mark levels QWK ≥0.75, within ±1 ≥85%, AO-level agreement ≥80%; 20–30-mark essays QWK ≥0.70, within ±2 ≥85%, AO4 level agreement ≥80% — **and** (b) QWK ≥ the measured human–human QWK on the same Tier-1 gold items; plus rationale validity ≥95%, signed error within ±0.3/10, run-to-run identical total ≥95% and identical level 100%, and no fairness bucket (region, L1, handwritten, length) lagging overall QWK by >0.05. Where the human line is not yet measured, (a) applies alone and every Mark carries the **provisional** badge. QWK 0.80 (8–12) / 0.78 (20-mark) are the published stretch targets and the point at which the provisional badge drops. (Ofqual's 0.52–0.96 figures are grade probabilities, not QWK; they justify measuring our own line, not lowering floors.) Below gate: feedback and spans show; **no number, no predicted grade** [A22]. *Seeding/auto-pause:* 2–5% of production traffic re-injects gold scripts; a question family breaching tolerance pauses within one batch, falls back to the second family with the provisional badge, and pages the content lead (GM§A4.4). *Monthly bias audit* per model and paper; the calibration page publishes QWK, level agreement, signed error, n, series and fairness buckets per paper — PROVISIONAL on [E7] externally, FINAL in-product.

**8.6 Ensembles and challenge-a-mark.** Cross-family second mark (Opus 5 primary, GPT-5.6 Sol second) on **100% of levels items ≥12 marks for the first two exam series per paper**, then 20% + all disputes once gated (JUDGING 15); disagreement >1 level → third family (Fable 5.1) → human queue [A23]. Challenge-a-mark: one tap, re-mark by a different family, side-by-side rationale, escalate to teacher/human; outcome logged to gold; targets challenge <3%, upheld <30%.

**8.7 Predicted grades.** Component raw → last-three-series thresholds → PUM → option grade; a range with the series used, spread and "marks to next grade" per component; A* only at option level; labelled "practice estimate, not a board mark"; unlocked only when the paper is gated, ≥3 series of thresholds are loaded and the student has ≥3 marked Attempts on that paper; surfaced on Progress › Paper and the mock debrief only; nothing flows into a school-recorded grade without teacher confirmation [A24][C11].

**8.8 Question generation.** Constrained by tariff, the paper's AO split, command word and insert conventions; emits a structured board-format scheme in our wording; passes style-similarity, independent-model solvability (≥98%) and near-duplicate checks; never reproduces real paper text; "5 more like this" holds tariff and cognitive-demand tag fixed; teacher rejection <10% [A27].

**8.9 Timed papers.** Minutes-per-mark from Pack metadata, extra-time % and rest breaks as Course settings every timer inherits, predict-before and postdict-after; the Principal-Examiner debrief is generated only from marked Attempts and the ER corpus: lost-mark clusters, ER phrases, historical threshold context, three scheduled retrieval tasks [A26].

**8.10 Handwriting pipeline.** Photo → VLM transcription (Gemini 3.1 Pro / 3.8 Flash; Mathpix for exact maths) → editable confirmation with low-confidence tokens underlined → mark from transcript *and* image; illegible tokens flagged never guessed; diagram items "provisional — teacher confirm" until a ≥300-item gold set exists; original image kept [A25].

**8.11 Teacher overrides feed calibration.** Every override (≤2 clicks) stores teacher id, reason code and both marks, enters Tier 2 gold after a second teacher or examiner confirms, and is reported as a disagreement trend per paper per quarter; double-marking teachers are credited. Anything feeding a school-recorded grade requires teacher confirmation — also the Annex III human-oversight posture [A55][C11].

**8.12 Scope by paradigm.** Expert-marked boards use the two engines; machine-scored exams (SAT, UNT, ЕГЭ, JEE) get an IRT engine in v2; IB IA/EE get criteria coaching, teacher marks first [A28]. English literature and Psychology essays are excluded from MVP (GM§B4c).
